## Lab Research Focus
The CAPR Lab explores how the human mind processes information, assesses risk, and makes decisions in complex, fast-changing environments. Our work bridges fundamental cognitive science with practical, real-world applications across several key domain areas.

---

## Research Topics

* **Human Behavior in Fire Emergencies:** We apply cognitive science to understand how individuals perceive fire intensity and make protective action decisions in building and wildland urban interface (WUI) fires. We are interested in how people think when they are alerted to fire hazards, such as via a fire alarm, and how they respond, such as when to use a fire extinguisher. This work aims to close the gap between fire safety engineering and the cognitive science of human behavior for the next generation of fire safety systems. 
  * *Relevant Publications:* 
    * [Bonny & Russell (2025) *Safety*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_role_2025' | relative_url }})
    * [Bonny, Hussain, Russell, Trouvé, & Milke (2024) *Frontiers in Psychology*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_simulated_2024' | relative_url }})
    * [Bonny & Milke (2023) *Fire*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_precision_2023' | relative_url }})
* **Human-Machine Teaming and Team Dynamics:** We examine how psychological traits, interpersonal coordination, and member behaviors shape cohesion and performance in human teams, as well as how humans interact with, trust, and collaborate alongside autonomous agents and machine teammates. 
  * *Relevant Publications:*
    * [Bonny & Wynne (2024) *ICHMS Proceedings*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_increasing_2024' | relative_url }})
    * [Bonny (2022) *Behavior Research Methods*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_self-report_2022' | relative_url }})
* **Cognitive Mechanisms of Non-Symbolic Magnitude Representation:** We study how the human mind estimates quantities, such as number, size, and area, without explicitly counting or calculating. Our research explores how these fundamental, intuitive magnitude systems develop and how they support higher-level mathematical abilities, geometric reasoning, and real-world estimation, such as fire intensity.
  * *Relevant Publications:*
    * [Bonny (2025) *Behavioral Sciences*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_seeing_2025' | relative_url }})
    * [Bonny & Lourenco (2013) *JECP*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_approximate_2013' | relative_url }})
* **Cognitive Predictors of Performance in Complex Environments:** We investigate how specific cognitive abilities, such as numeracy and personality traits, predict skill acquisition, team cohesion, and performance in high-tempo digital environments like multiplayer online video games.
  * *Relevant Publications:*
    * [Bonny (2024) *JEGE*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_individual_2024' | relative_url }})
    * [Bonny & Castaneda (2017) *Computers in Human Behavior*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_number_2017' | relative_url }})

## Lab Expertise & Research Techniques

The CAPR Lab utilizes rigorous, mixed-methods approaches to safely and accurately measure cognitive processes:

* **Simulation and Immersive Technologies:** Development and validation of high-fidelity virtual reality (VR) and extended reality (XR) environments to safely test decision-making and risk assessment in hazardous scenarios (e.g., residential fires). 
  * *E.g.:* [Russell, Bonny, & Reed (2024) *Fire*]({{ '/publications/#russell_impact_2024' | relative_url }})
* **Psychophysiological & Neurological Measurement:** Utilizing electroencephalography (EEG), and event-related potentials (ERPs) to capture the biological underpinnings of perception and cognition.
  * *E.g.:* [Bonny & Lourenco (2023) *Brain Sciences*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_electrophysiological_2023' | relative_url }})
* **Advanced Psychometrics:** Expertise in high-dimensional behavioral data analysis, longitudinal modeling, psychometrics, and collective metrics using R, Python, and MatLab.
  * *E.g.:* [Bonny & Wynne (2024) *ICHMS Proceedings*]({{ '/publications/#bonny_increasing_2024' | relative_url }})
* **Behavioral Task Programming:** Designing web-based and local behavioral tasks utilizing jsPsych, Unity, HTML/CSS, and JavaScript.
  * *E.g.:* [Bonny & Russell (2025) *Fire*]({{ '/publications/#bonny2025deciding' | relative_url }})
