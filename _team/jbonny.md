### About

[ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1601-5114) • [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Z5FPSFIAAAAJ) • [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/justin-bonny-78b20bb0)

Dr. Justin W. Bonny is an Associate Professor of Psychology at Morgan State University who specializes in cognitive science, developmental psychology, and human factors research. He earned his Ph.D. in Psychology with a concentration in Cognition and Development from Emory University.

### Bridging Theory and Application

Dr. Bonny’s career spans academic, defense, and industry research. He has served as a Research Scientist at foundry10, the **U.S. Department of the Navy (SSC LANT)** and later at **The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory (JHU/APL)**. This blend of academic and industry experience shapes the CAPR Lab’s mission: taking foundational insights about the human mind and applying them to real-world challenges.

Dr. Bonny's research has been sponsored federally, includeing NSF funding examining visual perception of fire cues and U.S. Army Research Institute funding to examine psychophysiological indicators of team cohesion.

### Mentorship & Academic Community

At Morgan State, Dr. Bonny works closely with undergraduate researchers as well as Master's and Doctoral students within the [**Graduate Program in Psychometrics**](https://www.morgan.edu/psychometrics). 

Beyond the lab, he serves on the Board of Directors for the [Eastern Psychological Association](https://www.easternpsychological.org/), mentors high school scholars, and serves on the Board of Directors for the [Ingenuity Project](https://www.ingenuityproject.org/).
