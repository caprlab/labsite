# Welcome to the CAPR Lab

The **Cognitive, Applied, and Perceptual Research (CAPR) Lab** is located in the [Department of Psychology at Morgan State University](https://www.morgan.edu/cla/psychology). Directed by Dr. Justin Bonny, the lab investigates human perception, cognition, human-computer interaction, and decision-making across simulated and real-world environments. 

The CAPR Lab applies cognitive science principles to investigate human perception, risk assessment, and decision-making across four primary research areas:

* **Fire Safety Science & Emergencies:** Examining pre-movement risk perception, visual cues, and decision thresholds during building and wildland fires.
* **Human-Autonomy Teaming & Dynamics:** Investigating trust, interpersonal coordination, and member behaviors in human teams and human-machine collaborations.
* **Non-Symbolic Magnitude Representation:** Studying how intuitive estimates of quantity, area, and dynamic magnitudes support spatial and mathematical reasoning and influence decision-making in sports betting and gambling environments.
* **Cognitive Predictors of Performance:** Identifying how individual cognitive abilities and personality traits predict skill acquisition in complex digital environments.

To study these processes, we leverage extended reality (XR/VR) simulations, electrophysiology (EEG/ERPs), advanced psychometrics, and custom behavioral task programming.
